Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I pay for the exam?
Go to Payment section > Select Subject > Choose Razorpay, UPI, Card or NetBanking > Click Pay.
How long is my payment valid?
Your payment is valid for 7 days from the date of successful payment. You can attempt exam within 7 days.
How to start the exam?
Go to My Exams > Complete Camera Verification first > Click "Start Exam" button at scheduled time.
What is camera verification?
Camera verification is mandatory to verify your identity before starting the exam. It prevents cheating.
How will I get my result?
Result will be available in Results section within 24 hours of exam completion. You can also download it.
Payment deducted but exam not showing?
Please wait 15 minutes. Check Payment History. If still not updated, contact support with Transaction ID.
Can I reattempt the exam?
No, each subject exam can be attempted only once. For reattempt, contact admin.
What if internet disconnects during exam?
Your answers are auto-saved. Reconnect and login again to continue. If time is over, contact support.
