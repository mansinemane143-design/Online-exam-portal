Frequently Asked Questions

How to create a new exam?
Go to Exam Management > Create Exam > Add Subject, Date, Time > Add Questions > Publish.
How to upload questions in bulk?
Go to Question Bank > Bulk Upload > Download CSV Template > Fill and Upload.
How to view student results?
Go to Reports > Select Exam > View All Student Scores and Download Result Sheet.
Can I edit exam after publishing?
No, once published you cannot edit. You can duplicate the exam and make changes.
